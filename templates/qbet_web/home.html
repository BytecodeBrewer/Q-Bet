User-controlled authority

Automation is a setting, not a hidden assumption.

Q-Bet separates finding a valid opportunity from deciding how much authority the system has to act on it.

Current

Notify only

Surface the opportunity and bring the user back to Q-Bet or a manual provider action.

Current

Approval before action

Require an explicit human decision before a supported controlled action can proceed.

Planned

Bounded automation

Allow configured actions only inside explicit risk, capital and provider limits.

Future

Higher automation

More authority may be delegated later when execution isolation and controls are mature.

Risk warning: removing human approval allows configured actions to execute without an individual confirmation step. Strict limits and monitoring are required.
